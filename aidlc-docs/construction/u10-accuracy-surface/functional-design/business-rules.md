## Why these choices

- **Point-to-plane ICP on the true surface.** Matching scan points to the nearest
  point *on a triangle* (not to sample points of the reference) means a perfect scan
  aligns to zero error; matching to samples would leave an error of about half the
  sample spacing, too large for the 0.05 mm proof.
- **A box tree, not a grid**, for nearest-triangle search: the reference has huge flat
  triangles beside tiny ones (see `SurfaceIndex`).
- **Coverage decides "another object".** Trimming lets almost any shape find some
  close points; only the right object covers most of the reference.
- **Underside excluded from coverage.** Nothing on the object overhangs more than
  45° (measurement sheet), so only the face it stands on points down; it is never
  scanned and would otherwise cost about a quarter of the coverage.
